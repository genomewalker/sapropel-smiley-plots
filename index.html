Sapropel smiley plots

Ancient-DNA damage estimates for bacterial, archaeal and viral reference genomes in sediment samples from eastern Mediterranean cores GeoB15103-1, -2 and -3: sapropels S1, S3, S5 and S6 and non-sapropel intervals, 0.7–173 ka. Damage was estimated with metaDMG from competitively mapped metagenomic reads.

Smiley plot

After death, cytosines near the ends of DNA fragments deaminate. Sequenced reads from old DNA therefore show C→T substitutions concentrated at the 5′ end and, on the complementary strand, G→A substitutions at the 3′ end.

  • The left half shows C→T frequency at positions 1–25 from the 5′ end; the right half shows G→A frequency at positions 25–1 towards the 3′ end.
  • Markers are the observed frequencies; lines are the fitted damage curve.
  • Frequencies that rise steeply towards both ends indicate damaged DNA. A flat profile indicates little or no damage.

Explorer

  1. Choose a sample. The sample list is ordered by age; sapropel layers carry a dark band.
  2. Narrow the taxonomy. Select a segment of the sunburst, or an entry in the list below it, to restrict the table to that group. Segment size is proportional to read count.
  3. Open a reference genome. A table row opens its smiley plot, the metaDMG fit and the coverage statistics (breadth, expected breadth, their ratio, Gini, entropy).
  4. Compare. Follow one species or genus through every sample, or put several samples side by side.

The address bar always describes the current view and can be shared, for example explorer/?sample=GeoB15103-1_19-34cm&taxon=GCF_000759875.1. Parameters are listed in Help.

Data files

The explorer reads these files directly in the browser with DuckDB-WASM. They can be downloaded and queried with any Parquet reader.

FileContents
meta.parquet One row per sample and reference genome: taxonomy, read counts, metaDMG damage fit, coverage statistics.
freq.parquet Per-position substitution frequencies and counts for the same rows (positions 1–25 from each read end).
samples.json Sample names, core, sapropel layer and age.
config.json Damage threshold, filter settings and build identifier.
data/README.md Column descriptions and example queries.
data/schema.json Column names and types, machine-readable.
llms.txt Plain-text description of the site, files and URL parameters for scripts and AI agents.
